This policy explains how [Operator name] (“we”, “us”) handles information when you play the mobile game “Neon Circuit” (the “Game”).

1. Summary

  • The Game has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or payment details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to our servers.
  • For ads (Google AdMob) and usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics), device information such as your advertising ID may be sent to Google, according to the choices you make on the consent screen.

  • Analytics data is deleted automatically by Google after the Firebase retention period ([2 or 14 months]).
  • Advertising data is retained according to Google's policies.
  • Support emails are kept for [1 year] after we reply, then deleted.

6. Your choices

  • Use the consent screen (Google User Messaging Platform) shown when you first start the Game.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”.

If you decline, you can still play; you may see non-personalized ads.
